## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-01

First release.
- Two new Quicksilver-shop cockpit figurines: Boo (sheet ghost with a pail of sweets) and Lantern Jack
  (pumpkin-headed skeleton holding up a glowing lantern, leaning on a crooked staff).
- Flickering candle-lit jack-o'-lanterns and lantern.
- Cursed-frigate green haze and glowing motes around each figurine.
- Wobbling heads, like the game's own figurines.
