[center][size=6][b]Halloween Figurines[/b][/size]
[size=4]Boo, Skelly and Pumpkin-Head for your cockpit dashboard[/size][/center]

Three [b]new[/b] cockpit figurines in the Quicksilver shop. None of the game's figurines are replaced.

[list]
[*][b]Boo[/b] - a see-through sheet ghost waving hello, with a glowing jack-o'-lantern pail of sweets
[*][b]Skelly[/b] - a little skeleton sitting on a big grinning jack-o'-lantern, eyes glowing cursed green
[*][b]Pumpkin-Head[/b] - a scarecrow in a tattered cloak with an angry carved head, straw hands and a lit lantern
[/list]

Every figurine comes with little carved jack-o'-lanterns whose candles flicker, and a [b]cursed green haze[/b] with glowing motes rolling around the dashboard. It's the same ghost-light the game uses on its haunted frigates, shrunk down to fit your cockpit. The heads wobble like the game's own figurines.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the [b]HalloweenFigurines[/b] folder into [i]No Man's Sky\GAMEDATA\MODS\[/i]
[*]Buy the figurines for 3,200 Quicksilver each from the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly.
[*]Install one in your starship's inventory like any other figurine.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Shop, technology, reward and product changes are merge files that only [b]add[/b] entries, so other mods that change those tables keep working.
[*]Shop overhauls that replace the whole Quicksilver list (such as ~Consumerism) must load [b]before[/b] this mod: in [i]Binaries\SETTINGS\GCMODSETTINGS.MXML[/i] give HALLOWEENFIGURINES a lower ModPriority number (the lowest number loads last and wins).
[*]The cockpit figurine list is a complete file. It also includes the figurines from [b]Box-Bot - Cockpit Robot Figurine[/b], so the two mods work together. Other mods that replace the figurine list will conflict.
[*]Other players without the mod won't see the figurines.
[/list]
