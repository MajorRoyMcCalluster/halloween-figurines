# Changelog

Only published releases are listed here.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-29

First release.
- Three new Quicksilver-shop cockpit figurines: Boo (sheet ghost with a pail of sweets), Skelly (skeleton on a
  jack-o'-lantern) and Pumpkin-Head (cloaked scarecrow with a lantern).
- Flickering candle-lit jack-o'-lanterns, glowing cursed-green skeleton eyes.
- Cursed-frigate green haze and glowing motes around each figurine.
- Wobbling heads, like the game's own figurines.
