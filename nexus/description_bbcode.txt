[size=3][i]A sheet ghost and a lantern-bearing pumpkin-head for your dashboard.[/i][/size][/center]

[line]

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]What it does[/b][/color][/size]
Adds two [b]new[/b] cockpit figurines to the Quicksilver shop. None of the game's figurines are replaced. They sit on the usual glowing figurine pedestal on your dashboard, and their heads wobble on the figurine's bobble joint as your ship moves.

[list]
[*][b]Boo[/b] - a see-through sheet ghost waving hello, with a glowing jack-o'-lantern pail of sweets
[*][b]Lantern Jack[/b] - a skeleton with a grinning jack-o'-lantern head, holding up a glowing lantern and leaning on a crooked staff. His aged bones glow cursed green in the cracks, even in a dark cockpit
[*]Candle-lit jack-o'-lanterns that flicker
[*]A [b]cursed green haze[/b] with glowing motes rolling round the dash - the ghost-light the game uses on its haunted frigates, shrunk to cockpit size
[*]Original models and textures, built from scratch for this mod
[/list]

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]Requirements[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]No other mods needed
[*]3,200 Quicksilver per figurine, spent at the [b]Quicksilver Synthesis Companion[/b] on the Space Anomaly
[/list]

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the [b]HalloweenFigurines[/b] folder into [b]No Man's Sky\GAMEDATA\MODS\[/b] (create the MODS folder if you don't have one).
[*]Buy the figurines from the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly (near the end of the shop).
[*]Install one in your starship's inventory like any other figurine. Your cockpit shows one figurine at a time.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]Uninstalling[/b][/color][/size]
Remove the figurines from your ship first, then delete the [b]HalloweenFigurines[/b] folder.

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]New items only[/b] - the shop, technology, reward and product changes are merge files that only [b]add[/b] entries, so other mods that change those tables keep working.
[*][b]Shop overhauls[/b] that replace the whole Quicksilver list (such as ~Consumerism) must load [b]before[/b] this mod: in [b]Binaries\SETTINGS\GCMODSETTINGS.MXML[/b] give HALLOWEENFIGURINES a lower ModPriority number (the lowest number loads last and wins).
[*][b]Conflicts[/b] with other mods that replace the cockpit figurine list. It already includes the figurines from [b]Box-Bot - Cockpit Robot Figurine[/b], so the two work together.
[*][b]Multiplayer[/b] - other players without the mod won't see the figurines.
[*]Names and descriptions are in English and US English.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#ff8a1f][b]FAQ[/b][/color][/size]
[b]I can't find them in the shop.[/b]
They're near the end of the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion's list. If you use a shop overhaul, check the load order above, then restart the game.

[b]The figurine vanished from my ship.[/b]
The mod isn't loading (or was removed), so the game drops items it doesn't know. Check the folder path is GAMEDATA\MODS\HalloweenFigurines\METADATA... (not MODS\HalloweenFigurines\HalloweenFigurines\...), then buy it again.
